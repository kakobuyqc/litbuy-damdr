The method / 02

A practical Litbuy shopping guide.

A useful shortlist answers three questions: What is the item? Will it fit your needs? What will it cost to receive? Keep the evidence alongside the link so you can compare options later.

  1. Browse with a clear purpose

    Choose a category first. Decide whether you need everyday sneakers, a lightweight jacket, matching sets or small accessories before collecting Litbuy finds.

  2. Record the exact listing

    Save the source URL, seller name, color and selected variant. A product name alone may refer to several different versions.

  3. Compare fit and construction

    Read measurements and material information. Check relevant photos and reviews, and mark missing details as unknown instead of filling them with assumptions.

  4. Recheck before you pay

    Confirm availability, service compatibility, delivery charges and return conditions. Treat saved prices and shipping estimates as notes that need a fresh check.

The comparison sheet / 03

Better columns. Clearer choices.

Use this structure in your own worksheet to keep product comparisons consistent.

Suggested fields for your fashion research spreadsheet
Research field What to record Why it helps
Product identity Original link, seller, category, color and variant Keeps similar-looking listings separate.
Clothing fit Chest, shoulder, length, waist and inseam where relevant Makes a Litbuy clothing spreadsheet useful beyond size labels.
Shoe sizing Size system, seller chart and foot-length guidance Gives a Litbuy shoes spreadsheet a consistent basis for comparison.
Materials & details Fabric composition, lining, hardware and care instructions Separates documented information from visual impressions.
Evidence Available product photos, review notes and unanswered questions Shows what you have checked and what still needs clarification.
Cost & conditions Price, currency, shipping quote, fees and return terms Supports a comparison of total costs rather than item prices alone.

Budget with the full total: item price + applicable domestic delivery + service fees + international shipping + any import charges. Use current quotes, keep currencies consistent and avoid adding charges twice.

Quick answers / 05

Before you shortlist.

Clear answers to common questions about spreadsheet research, product links and purchase planning.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a worksheet for organizing product research associated with Litbuy shopping. Useful columns include product links, category, size, measurements, listed price and review notes. This page provides a guide and CAFANS category links, rather than a downloadable spreadsheet.

How do I use Litbuy product links?

Open the original listing and check the exact variant, seller information and current purchase conditions. If using a shopping service, confirm that it accepts the original link before paying. CAFANS links on this page open CAFANS browsing pages.

What belongs in a Litbuy clothing spreadsheet?

Record garment measurements, fabric information, color, size, seller link, listed price and review notes. Compare measurements with a garment you already own instead of relying only on letter sizes.

How should I compare shoes?

Compare the exact model and variant, the seller's size chart, foot-length guidance, materials, available photos and return conditions. A familiar size label alone does not confirm fit.

Does a spreadsheet price include shipping and taxes?

Do not assume that a listed price includes shipping or taxes. Build a total from the item price, applicable domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and any import charges, using current quotes.

Is this an official Litbuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent shopping research page directing visitors to CAFANS. Brand photographs are editorial references and do not establish stock availability, authenticity, affiliation or endorsement.
